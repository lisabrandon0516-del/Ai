Retail Rivals

1. Unzip this whole folder. Keep Play.bat next to the open_late folder.
2. Install Python 3.11 or newer from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   On the installer, check Add python.exe to PATH.
3. Double-click Play.bat. The first launch installs the desk and can take a minute.

On the first screen, pick Shop, Online, or Corporate office.
Corporate office is the chain: the Evansville warehouse, then Seattle, Houston, Las Vegas, and Naperville.

Saves stay on this computer, in your user folder under .open_late.
